    GaugeRoPE: RoPE-Compatible Executable Latent Attention
    Yuanhang Gao, Xiangrui Yang, Lingyuan Meng, and 2 more authors
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2611.20524, Nov 2026
    Abs arXiv PDF

    GaugeRoPE is a training-free executable latent KV representation for RoPE-based transformers. It preserves the rotary action in a RoPE-compatible carrier and uses a residual key–value correction, avoiding per-token dense historical K/V reconstruction. On Mistral-7B, it achieves 5.85x KV compression with quality close to the full-cache baseline and up to 2.48x decode throughput over the uncompressed baseline.

  1. TSC CCF-A
    Magneto: Load-Balanced Key-Value Service for Write-Intensive Workloads
    Yuanhang Gao, Yingwen Chen, Xiangrui Yang, and 3 more authors
    IEEE Transactions on Services Computing, 2025
    Abs DOI Bib PDF

    High-performance key-value storage is critical for cloud applications, but write-intensive and highly skewed workloads can cause load imbalance among storage servers. Magneto introduces an in-switch delayed-write mechanism that absorbs frequent writes to hot items, balances load without a centralized controller, and includes a reliability mechanism for switch transitions and failures. An FPGA-integrated-switch prototype achieves up to 8.4x system throughput over baseline systems and reduces backend-server load to 56 percent of the baseline on a skewed workload with 70 percent reads and 30 percent writes.

  2. CJC CCF-A
    CAInNet: In-Network Computing Model for AI Acceleration
    Zhongpei Liu, Xiangrui Yang, Ling Yang, and 4 more authors
    Chinese Journal of Computers, 2025
    In Chinese
    Abs DOI PDF

    CAInNet is a computing-network integrated in-network computing model for AI acceleration. It combines SIMD and MIMD execution modes with a programmable data plane, enabling protocol-independent packet processing and in-network computation for AI inference and training data. An implementation uses in-band network telemetry and an MLP-based packet classifier, achieving 98.3 percent accuracy with 5k routing entries while saving 98.7 percent of storage space without additional programmable data-plane latency.

  1. ISPA CCF-C
    Optimizing In-network Caching for Key-Value Stores under Write-intensive Workloads
    Yuanhang Gao, Yingwen Chen, Xiangrui Yang, and 4 more authors
    In 2024 IEEE International Symposium on Parallel and Distributed Processing with Applications (ISPA), 2024
    Abs PDF

    Key-value stores suffer from performance degradation under write-intensive workloads because frequent writes invalidate cached data. Freq-absorb introduces delayed writes in the switch data plane, buffering write queries before committing them to backend servers. An FPGA-based top-of-rack switch prototype reduces switch misses from 99 percent to 48.4 percent under write-intensive workloads, improving cache hit rates and system performance.
